Deal Or No Deal - Advanced Programming (High School)

This was the final project (ISU) of my Advanced Programming Class from High School. This is a replica of the real life game Deal or No Deal. In this game the user has to choose a case that they believe has the highest amount of money that they can win i.e. a million dollars and slowly eliminate every other case until two remain. Then they get to choose whether or not they want to swap their case or keep the one they have. During the course of the game, they also get offers from the banker to leave the game and keep the money the banker offers.

#### Spotify

This was more of a personal Project. The project itself is a music player. The reason I created this application was because of Spotify itself. On Spotify there are a lot of ads and also the premium version is pretty expensive. This was the reason I made this app. The app uses .wav files of the music since it uses java 1.4.
